# E HUD 0.1.3-alpha

## Focused View

E HUD now stays focused on what you are actually looking at.

- The default inspection range now follows your player's normal block-interaction reach.
- You can still increase the range in **Settings → Performance & Backups**.
- Added an 8-block option for a small custom range.
- Longer custom ranges are capped by your simulation distance.
- If you are looking at empty space, E HUD stays hidden instead of showing an unrelated target card.

This is an alpha release for Fabric. Please report anything odd in the Oolist Discord.
